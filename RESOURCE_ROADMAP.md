# Resources roadmap

- Locations: include Desert and Winstrate House as distinct subsections within Route 111, rather than standalone list entries. Keep Berry Master’s House as a standalone location.
- Include Pokémon Trainers’ School within Rustboro City and Pretty Petal Flower Shop within Route 104. Keep Battle Frontier and all seven facilities as standalone locations; link future facility guides to those pages rather than duplicating them.

- Defer Trainer Hill coverage for now; consider adding its trainers, teams, challenge modes, and rewards later.
- Plan a Battle Frontier database page within Resources for reference content. This would be separate from the main app's Battle Frontier page.
- Keep the trainer database Emerald-only. Other-game reference content should support obtaining Pokémon or moves for use in Emerald rather than expanding into unrelated trainer databases.

## Deferred encounter presentation idea
Shared encounter stats followed by categorized location lists were trialed on Krabby (FireRed) and Wailmer, then reverted because the multi-pattern lists felt too busy. Revisit a similar approach later if it can preserve exact data and be easier to scan. Keep the existing exact-match grouping and individually approved summaries for now.
